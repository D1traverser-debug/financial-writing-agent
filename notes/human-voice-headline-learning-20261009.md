## External craft sources reviewed
- Nieman Storyboard — nonfiction workshop notes: precision, openings as contracts, ladder of abstraction.
  https://niemanstoryboard.org/2023/10/26/notes-from-a-nonfiction-writing-workshop/
- Nieman Storyboard — reporting/writing scenes: selective detail, camera position, pacing, understatement.
  https://niemanstoryboard.org/2023/04/20/narrative-journalism-reporting-writing-scenes/
- Nieman Storyboard — ladder of abstraction: move between concrete detail and larger meaning.
  https://niemanstoryboard.org/2023/05/31/ladder-of-abstraction-narrative-writing-details-theme-empathy/
- Nieman Storyboard — natural narratives: a central question can create tension, but withholding must not become coyness.
  https://niemanstoryboard.org/2007/02/16/natural-narratives/
- Poynter — stronger headlines: specific, accurate, curiosity without misleading/overpromising.
  https://www.poynter.org/educators-students/2017/9-tips-for-writing-stronger-headlines/
- Nielsen Norman Group — headline/web information scent and front-loading.
  https://www.nngroup.com/articles/worlds-best-headlines-bbc-news/
- Scientific Reports (2025) — 8,977 headline experiments: headline concreteness has a curvilinear relationship with selection; excessive vagueness or excessive completeness can both underperform.
  https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-81575-9
- Applied Cognitive Psychology (2024) — >100,000 WeChat news articles: salient questions, importance and surprisingness can predict clicks; curiosity-driven clicks do not automatically imply stronger downstream satisfaction.
  https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/acp.4195
