## 5. Independent external-method study and practical adaptation

1. **Reuters journalistic standards** https://reutersagency.com/about/standards-values/ : prefer traceable named first-hand sources and fair attribution, protect editorial independence. APPLY to financial claim register, quote accuracy, rumor-vs-official stage; do not copy a newsroom organizational approval hierarchy into one Agent.
2. **AP newsroom standards** https://www.ap.org/about/news-values-and-principles/telling-the-story/ : verify claims, sources, originality, precise corrections, graphics/data integrity; connect corrections to article versions and actual recipients. APPLY as negative tests and evidence handling; not proof a model's same-chat audit is independent.
3. **SPJ ethics** https://www.spj.org/spj-code-of-ethics/ : seek truth, contextualize, verify and update. APPLY as principles, not a new 35-item prompt blacklist.
4. **Google people-first/YMYL** https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content : finance content has heightened trust stakes, readers need reliable who/how/why; E-E-A-T itself is NOT a scalar Google ranking factor. APPLY to product trust/usability and author transparency, NOT optimize for a fabricated ranking score.
5. **NIST GenAI RMF Profile** https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-generative-artificial-intelligence : control confident falsehood, provenance errors, over-trust in hallucinated citations and model self-evals. APPLY risk-based layered tests with clear assertion/observation distinction.
6. **China CAC financial information services rules** https://www.cac.gov.cn/2018-12/26/c_1123908386.htm : source traceability, content review and information integrity; provider licensing applicability is legal/factual-scope dependent. APPLY as a compliance review area, not a claim this particular writing service is or isn't licensed.
7. **China CSRC unlicensed stock-tip warning** https://www.csrc.gov.cn/csrc/c100028/c1002385/content.shtml : don't conflate analytical reporting with sell-side instruction or disguised paid stock tips; verify business-model/legal specifics for deployment.
8. **SEI ATAM / architecture tradeoff lens** https://www.sei.cmu.edu/library/architecture-tradeoff-analysis-method-collection/ : compare product outcomes and reliability/modifiability/latency, not number of prohibitions.
9. **Prior internal methodology** `evals/FINANCIAL_FULL_SYSTEM_REBUILD_2026-10-12.md` and `evals/FINANCIAL_CONTROL_PLANE_REASSESSMENT_20261017.md`: existing DDD/single-aggregate, phase and gate work is reusable and has to be rechecked against current code; avoid parallel "new Writing OS".

Research qualification: these are separately studied method families from primary source standards, public documentation and inspected source contracts. **No claim of exhausting the web or attaining comprehensive study saturation**: additional empirical news/product writing methods and full live applicability tests remain open.
