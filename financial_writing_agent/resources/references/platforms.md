# Platform adaptation

## Ownership
This module owns **distribution-surface adaptation**. It does not weaken evidence standards, company-decision utility, Natural Authorial Prose or the Reader Funnel. `reader_value.md` owns the current three-product reader-value contract and overrides older platform assumptions when more specific.

Financial Writing supplies publication text/Word artifacts. It does not generate the user's later article illustrations; section-entry prose must leave image-safe insertion zones where lower-level headings follow.

## Current product routing
New no-target work defaults to `PORTFOLIO` so one audited evidence base can produce all products the user normally publishes:
- `FINANCE_DEEP_DOCX` — deep finance/review long-form;
- `FINANCE_BROAD_DOCX` — acquisition long-form;
- `PLATFORM_INTERACTION_PACK_DOCX` — standalone short image-text/micro/dynamic copy;
- `AUDIT_DOCX` — audit evidence.

`ACQUISITION` produces Broad + interaction pack + audit.

Historical compatibility is deliberate: legacy `DUAL` remains Deep + Broad + audit, and legacy `FINANCE_BROAD` remains Broad + audit. Existing article-state contracts are not retroactively expanded.

## Platform map

### Deep finance / review long-form
Primary destinations:
- 韭研公社
- 东方财富 / 东方财富号
- 微信公众号

Reader job: complete finance review, material-company coverage, explicit evidence-bounded company differentiation, realization/sensitivity comparison and validation/falsification logic.

### Acquisition long-form
Primary destinations:
- 微信公众号
- 头条号 / 今日头条
- 百家号
- 网易号
- 企鹅号 / 企鹅媒体平台

Reader job: attract cold finance-domain readers, earn continuation/completion, and convert useful understanding into follower acquisition. Broad is **not** a shortened Deep article. It is rebuilt from the same audited evidence around one to three strongest finance-wide gaps.

### Interaction short-form pack
Primary destinations:
- 微信公众号贴图文字/短内容
- 微头条 / 头条动态
- 百家号动态
- 网易号动态

Reader job: standalone interaction and follower acquisition. Each item is independently rewritten from the audited evidence base; copying or truncating a long-form paragraph is not platform adaptation.

The short pack should normally contain multiple platform-family variants with:
- an information-bearing first-line hook;
- one compact fact → financial consequence → company/logic distinction chain;
- a natural interaction prompt or question that invites a view without baiting or giving trading instructions;
- source privacy and the same factual/uncertainty boundaries as long-form outputs.

## WeChat is a dual-role surface
微信公众号 is deliberately both a Deep and Broad destination for this user. A WeChat-only target therefore resolves to `PORTFOLIO`, not one arbitrarily chosen long-form mode. The user may publish the two long-form versions for different account/content jobs and also reuse the interaction pack for shorter image-text engagement.

## FINANCE_DEEP adaptation
Preserve material completeness. Do not widen reach by deleting the evidence or company distinctions that matter to a financially literate judgment.

When multiple MATERIAL companies exist, preserve the Company Decision Map: which exposure is more direct/pure, where realization is faster or harder, what evidence is stronger, where elasticity may be higher but less certain, and what changes the comparison. This remains non-actionable analysis rather than buy/sell guidance.

## FINANCE_BROAD adaptation
Broad is an acquisition product for finance readers, including readers with no prior interest in the niche sector. Surface the financial stake early: profit, orders, cash flow, price/volume, company differentiation, policy transmission, capital allocation or another evidence-backed money consequence.

Select one to three strongest information gaps. Rebuild title, first-three-sentence opening, H1 sequence and completion payoff around those gaps. Do not merely delete paragraphs from Deep.

## WeChat public-account long-form
WeChat discovery can come from subscription, recommendation/social circulation and Search. Treat click/recommendation value and search intent as related but independent signals.

### Title adaptation
A title must first match the target reader and promised payoff. For Broad, cold-finance-reader relevance is mandatory; for Deep, a narrower qualified-finance framing remains legal when it better matches the actual payoff.

Use current search evidence when accessible, in this priority:
1. the user's own account search/source terms and performance;
2. current WeChat Search / WeChat Index / suggestions;
3. current Tencent/WeChat ecosystem evidence;
4. clearly labelled proxies.

Never invent exact search volume or call a term high-frequency without evidence. Search terms must remain natural; keyword stuffing fails.

### Mobile/readability adaptation
Use clear hierarchy, visible meaningful numbering, readable paragraph density, image-safe H1 lead paragraphs and a useful bold skim path. The known audience age profile is a readability prior, not a sophistication label.

## Toutiao / Baijiahao / broad-feed discipline
Current platform rules penalize exaggerated or misleading titles, hidden critical information, title-body mismatch, logic-disjoint or low-information stitched content. Strong acquisition packaging therefore means a more consequential truthful stake — not fabricated urgency or pure suspense.

Short-form variants should be native to the short surface: shorter causal chain, one clear judgment, one interaction prompt. They must not be the first few paragraphs of the long article with the rest cut off.

## Platform data discipline
Algorithms are changing external systems. Prefer current official/UI evidence and the account's own recent behavior. Separate:
- distribution/recommendation signals;
- search/long-tail signals;
- article-quality/retention signals;
- account-specific observed performance.

One viral or failed article is a sample, not a permanent formula.

## Post-publication learning
When post-publication data is available and materially useful, persist it through a supported owner evidence-capture action or another verified durable reference bound to the exact article workspace.

Useful metrics include platform, product/version, topic, title, publish time, article length, impressions/recommendations, reads, CTR/read-recommend rate, average read time, completion/effective rate, likes, comments, shares, favorites, follows, traffic-source mix, search share and recommendation share.

Use repeated comparable samples to update hypotheses. Do not promote one result into a rigid title, opening or structure formula.
