# Research — Cognition Before Thesis

Status: `CURRENT_CANDIDATE_1.0`
Owner: `DOMAIN:financial_writing`

## Purpose

Research exists to build the strongest evidence-bounded model of a topic before publication choices are made. It is broader than the final article. Research must not hide a material value-chain branch merely because the eventual article selects a narrower story.

## Mandatory choreography

For normal thematic/company work, current Research follows:

`prior cognition recall -> value-chain preflight -> company/universe recall -> current evidence expansion -> adversarial/disconfirming search -> search saturation -> thesis competition/adjudication -> company decision map -> research audit`

The order is semantic, not cosmetic.

### 1. Prior cognition recall

Retrieve relevant earned knowledge when available: repository-native prior Financial research/artifacts, approved/edited user material, durable corrections, connected knowledge stores and method knowledge. Treat recalled items as hypotheses/context, not current truth. Anything time-sensitive or material must be revalidated.

## Mandatory value-chain preflight

### 2. Value-chain preflight — mandatory before thesis selection

This is a **hard pre-thesis requirement**. Enumerate every economically material class that could transmit the event/theme into revenue, cost, margin, cash flow, assets, demand, regulation or competitive position.

For each class record:
- economic role;
- upstream/downstream relationships;
- transmission paths;
- evidence/source refs;
- unresolved or excluded classes and why.

No research package may call its thesis selected, settled or converged before this map exists. Thesis selection happens **after** the map exists, and only when the Value-Chain Coverage Map is COMPLETE or explicitly BOUNDED_WITH_GAPS under the declared scope.

`thesis_selection_started_after_preflight` must remain true. If a material class is discovered later, invalidate convergence and retest the thesis.

A prior chain map can accelerate this step but never waive it.

### 3. Company/universe recall

For every material value-chain class, search for listed-company candidates rather than beginning from familiar concept stocks. Account for included, excluded and unresolved candidates. Map every included company to a declared chain class.

For material companies retain positive and negative evidence, exposure type, benefit path, sensitivity basis, realization horizon, adverse variables and why-this/why-not reasoning.

### 4. Current evidence expansion

Prefer the strongest evidence available for the claim:
1. current first-party/regulatory/company disclosures;
2. authoritative primary datasets;
3. high-quality secondary reporting/research for discovery and context;
4. community/market discussion for hypotheses or sentiment, not as unquestioned fact.

Separate:
- `VERIFIED_FACT`;
- `SUPPORTED_MECHANISM`;
- `PLAUSIBLE_INTERPRETATION`;
- `UNRESOLVED`.

Current policy, tariffs, platform rules, financials, orders, guidance, market share, price/flow/attention and management language must be timestamped and revalidated whenever materially used.

## Comprehensive search, falsification and layered candidate selection

Operating definition: **broad multi-source recall + adversarial/disconfirming search + auditable saturation/gap boundaries**.

### 5. Adversarial/disconfirming search

Do not research only for evidence that supports the first thesis. At minimum perform materially distinct sweeps such as:
- **Negative/falsification sweep** — companies/facts that should fit but do not, reversal conditions, policy or operating evidence that weakens the preferred path;
- **Alternative-hypothesis sweep** — credible causal explanations that could account for the same evidence;
- **Cross-reference/snowball sweep** — follow material entities, disclosures, cited documents and adjacent chain nodes discovered in stronger sources.

Use a **challenge-and-eliminate** posture: for each leading thesis, build the strongest case against it before promotion. Counterevidence belongs in the durable research package; failed hypotheses are not erased when they influenced the final judgment.

### 6. Search breadth and saturation

"Global/full-web search" means broad, multi-source, adversarial search with an auditable stop rule — never an unverifiable claim that every page on the internet was crawled.

For a material thesis or method decision:
- use materially different source families;
- record each sweep and its new candidate, failure mode, counterexample and whether it changed the decision;
- continue when a sweep finds a decision-changing item;
- stop only after **two consecutive sweeps** add no decision-changing candidate, failure mode, counterexample or changed decision;
- retain unresolved gaps in the stop receipt.

A later material discovery invalidates saturation and requires re-adjudication.

### 7. Layered selection ladder

Do not promote the first plausible explanation. Move candidates through a Layered selection ladder:
1. discovered/recalled candidates;
2. economically plausible candidates;
3. evidence-supported candidates;
4. candidates surviving counterevidence and strongest-case-against review;
5. final adjudication under DecisionEnvelope.

If uncertainty reduction could plausibly change the ranking, obtain more evidence rather than pretending convergence.

For each credible candidate record mechanism, advantages, failure modes, fit/misfit conditions, counterexamples, rejected paths, selected rationale, verification plan and remaining unknowns.

Use `financial_writing_agent.cognition_governance.DecisionEnvelope` for material judgments. User feedback may trigger or inform a candidate, but cannot be the sole evidence for a material decision. If the judgment is problem-triggered or changes architecture/governance, external method/architecture research is additionally mandatory.

### 8. Company Decision Map

Two or more `MATERIAL` companies require a Company Decision Map. Dimensions are topic-specific, such as business purity, scale/mix, realization speed, margin sensitivity, evidence strength, capital intensity, inventory/FX exposure or adverse variables.

The map must end in a named, evidence-bounded comparative verdict and explicit conditions that would change it. It must not become buy/sell/timing/target/position advice.

## Coverage states

Use `COMPLETE` only when no known material class remains unresolved under the stated scope. Use `BOUNDED_WITH_GAPS` when material gaps remain and state them. `NOT_APPLICABLE` is legal only when value-chain/company mapping genuinely does not apply.

Research coverage requires at least two meaningful sweeps. A high source count is not equivalent to coverage if all sources repeat the same node or narrative.

## Market explanation

When market-price/leadership/cross-sectional behavior is material to the requested article, build competing market-explanation hypotheses and distinguish evidence from narrative. When it is not material, explicitly mark the scope `NOT_MATERIAL` rather than fabricating a market explanation.

## Output contract

Current Research must support:
- chronology / what changed;
- value-chain coverage map;
- transmission paths;
- company universe + coverage ledger;
- evidence ledger;
- counterevidence and unresolved gaps;
- candidate theses + Decision Envelope;
- Company Decision Map when applicable;
- market cognition/explanation when applicable;
- search saturation evidence;
- knowledge writeback candidates.

Research is complete only when these artifacts are sufficient for an adversarial Research Audit; source volume or prose length alone is not completion evidence.
