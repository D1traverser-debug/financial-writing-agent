# Editorial module

## Principle
Research is broad for every formal article. **FINANCE_DEEP and FINANCE_BROAD have different expression contracts.**

- `FINANCE_DEEP` is the comprehensive financial version. It must express every **material** branch that could change a financially literate reader's judgment. It may compress low-materiality repetition, but it must not omit a material event leg, transmission link, company class, counterexample, `why_this/why_not`, validation condition, market-explanation branch or risk merely to make the article shorter or cleaner.
- `FINANCE_BROAD` is the traffic-oriented broad-distribution version. It may select and compress around one to three core contradictions/information gaps so long as the retained chain remains factually complete enough to support its conclusions.

“Comprehensive” is bounded by the audited ResearchPackage, not by an impossible infinite universe. The deep article is material-complete relative to the bounded research universe.

Default formal output is Simplified Chinese financial prose.

## Shared TopicDNA
Before mode-specific structuring, determine:
- what genuinely changed;
- the main economic transmission chain;
- the most consequential reader misjudgment;
- which company-comparison dimensions matter for this topic;
- the reader-value / finance-wide question that makes the topic worth attention.

**Do not use a shared `core_contradictions` or shared `compression_candidates` field to govern FINANCE_DEEP.** Those selection devices belong only to the broad/traffic version.

## FINANCE_DEEP plan
The deep plan must declare explicit `completeness_obligations`: the material dimensions/branches that the final article must cover.

Typical obligations may include event chronology, industrial/economic transmission, relevant company categories, business-stage differences, financial realization, market-selection logic, counterevidence, validation/falsification and risks. The exact list is topic-specific.

When `ResearchPackage.market_explanation` is active, completeness obligations must additionally preserve every material branch needed to explain:
- who truly benefits economically and through what path;
- why exposure/sensitivity differs across material companies/classes;
- why market selection/price leadership can differ from fundamental benefit;
- which competing price-formation mechanisms remain plausible;
- what supports continuation versus what supports exhaustion/reversal;
- what remains unresolved and what evidence would falsify the favored interpretation.

The deep version may prioritize and sequence, but not selectively hide a material branch. If a material item is intentionally excluded, it must be named as an explicit research boundary rather than silently omitted.

### No hindsight-causality shortcut
A limit-up, strong return, high turnover, order imbalance, concept label, fund-flow proxy or continued advance is an **observed market fact**, not automatic proof of its own cause.

Market explanations must inherit the research confidence level:
- `VERIFIED_FACT` may be stated as fact;
- `SUPPORTED_MECHANISM` may be described as a supported mechanism, not a uniquely identified cause;
- `PLAUSIBLE_INTERPRETATION` must stay visibly conditional;
- `UNRESOLVED` must remain unresolved.

The article should prefer a transparent multi-cause explanation over a cleaner but invented single narrative.

## FINANCE_BROAD plan
The broad/traffic version may identify one to three `core_contradictions` and `compression_candidates`. It should lower entry cost and maximize qualified completion while preserving factual support and the causal chain needed for its conclusions.

If a broader finance-wide framing is genuinely material, use it to widen the audience. Do not force a cross-industry angle when the real value is sector-specific.

If it keeps a market-selection explanation, it must retain the key confidence boundary and counterevidence even when the surrounding universe is compressed.

Broad does **not** mean shallow, generic or inaccurate. It means a more selective route through the same evidence base.

## Information density
No default word-count target. Preserve independent facts, key numbers, causal steps, counterexamples, why-this/why-not, market-selection mechanism, validation/falsification conditions and risk boundaries according to the applicable mode contract.

For FINANCE_DEEP, compression may remove only repetition, textbook background, synonym restatement and immaterial detail. It must not remove material analytical or market-explanation branches.

For FINANCE_BROAD, additional selective compression is allowed when the retained article still fully supports its claims.

## Prose, numbering and continuity
Use continuous report-style prose, but use the formal hierarchy **frequently when the reasoning genuinely contains layers, steps, comparisons or categories**:
`一、二、三` → `（1）（2）（3）` → `①②③`.

“More numbering” means clearer visible structure, not mechanical “首先/其次/最后” filler. Paragraph transitions must still follow the actual causal/comparative/chronological/inferential logic.

Tables are not used in publication articles. Convert comparison material into numbered prose with explicit dimensions and conclusions.

Sections/paragraphs must have a visible relationship: cause, consequence, contrast, chronology, narrowing, comparison, correction or inference. Avoid abrupt topic jumps and stitched-block feel.

## Two article modes
### FINANCE_DEEP
For finance communities and readers seeking full financial analysis. Preserve the full bounded reasoning path and all material coverage obligations. Readers should be able to audit how the conclusion was derived, why some companies/market roles differ, which market explanations are stronger/weaker and what could invalidate them.

### FINANCE_BROAD
For broad-distribution / traffic acquisition while remaining a financial article. Reduce first-read cognitive cost and selectively emphasize the strongest supported route through the evidence. Broader cross-sector finance relevance is a tool when true, not a mandatory framing.

`DUAL` shares one research base but independently edits title, opening, structure and emphasis for each mode. It does **not** force the deep version to adopt the broad version's compression or audience strategy.

## Reader Funnel / Hook engine
Load `references/hooks.md`. Click, first-screen retention, H1/transition continuation and completion are one reader funnel, not independent tricks.

Formal applicable modes generate 8–12 headline candidates from genuinely different reader-value propositions and supported information gaps. Each candidate must identify the intended reader, financial relevance, target-reader click reason, promised payoff, current search terms/evidence and cognitive load. Broader-finance relevance is optional when genuinely useful.

The selected title is judged on audience-promise fit, information value, curiosity/concreteness balance and current WeChat search discoverability. Question titles are not assumed superior.

## Natural Authorial Prose / Writing DNA
Load `references/style.md`. The quality target is not detector evasion or superficial “humanization”; it is a consolidated system of fidelity, semantic contribution, argument progression, reader calibration, authorial selectivity, evidence specificity, natural structure/rhythm, template/redundancy control and calibrated rhetoric.

Writing DNA should learn from explicit user instruction, repeated accepted edits, stable user-corpus patterns and carefully abstracted external benchmarks. One-off edits or one external skill never become authority by themselves.

## Bold skim path
Keywords, key numbers and selected key judgment sentences should be bold so that **title + H1s + bold spans** reconstruct the article's main line for a fast reader. For stock/theme articles this skim path should include the essential company-comparison / market-selection conclusion boundary, not just the event itself. Do not over-bold.

## Risk section
The writer returns a separate `risk_disclaimer` field for each formal article. Do not place a risk/disclaimer H1 inside article blocks. Deterministic delivery appends the final section so it cannot be omitted or duplicated by formatting drift.

## Editorial judge
General editorial audit is independent from both the Reader Funnel / Hook Gate and Natural Authorial Prose / Writing DNA Gate. It checks evidence, mode-specific coverage/compression legality, market-explanation epistemic discipline where applicable, reasoning continuity, information density, reader motivation and compliance. PASS is illegal until both dedicated gates PASS.
