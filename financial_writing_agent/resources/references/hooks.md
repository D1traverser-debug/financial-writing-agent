# Hook and completion module

## Objective
Optimize the full reader funnel for publishable financial writing: **open → stay through the first screen → keep moving through H1/paragraph transitions → finish the article**. Hooks must increase attention without weakening factual support or turning into deceptive clickbait.

The primary audience profile currently supplied by the user skews older: readers around 45 are common and 55+ form a substantial share. Mobile reading therefore prioritizes strong signposting, low avoidable cognitive load, visible hierarchy and a meaningful bold skim path. Do not patronize the reader or dilute analytical depth.

## Dedicated Hook Gate
Hook quality is an independent semantic gate. It is not merged into the general editorial audit or the human-voice audit. The frozen draft must be judged on all dimensions below.

### 1. Total-title cross-finance clickability
A title must give **finance readers outside the article's immediate sector** a reason to click. A semiconductor-focused investor should still be able to see why an energy, pharma, auto or chemical story matters financially.

Prefer supported finance-wide stakes such as profit, pricing, valuation, orders, cash flow, supply/demand, policy transmission, capital allocation, market selection, earnings timing or another material economic consequence. Keep the topic/entity identifiable; do not hide the sector merely to look broad.

This is an independent condition from search-keyword optimization.

### 2. WeChat search-intent condition
Before title selection, perform current keyword reconnaissance instead of relying on a permanent remembered keyword list. Prefer, when accessible:
1. the user's own WeChat/public-account search-source terms;
2. current WeChat Search / WeChat Index / search-suggestion evidence;
3. current Tencent/WeChat ecosystem evidence;
4. clearly labeled current proxies when direct evidence is unavailable.

Record the actual candidate terms and evidence references. Do not invent exact search volume or call a term “high-frequency” without current evidence. Place useful terms naturally in title/lead/body when they improve search discoverability; never keyword-stuff or sacrifice clickability/readability.

### 3. First-three-sentence retention
Treat the first three complete sentences as a specific retention surface. They should, in a natural order rather than a rigid template:
- deliver a concrete fact/change/payoff quickly;
- make clear why a finance reader should care;
- create a supported forward question, tension or information gap that the body will answer.

Do not spend the first screen on generic background, throat-clearing, self-introduction or a rephrased headline.

### 4. H1 continuation power
Every H1 is a mini-hook and navigation promise. It must be topic-specific, information-bearing and make the next section worth entering. Avoid generic headings such as “产业链梳理”, “相关公司”, “市场表现”, “后续展望” unless the wording itself carries a specific finding/question.

### 5. Paragraph forward pull
Transitions must perform a real job: cause, consequence, contrast, chronology, narrowing, comparison, correction or inference. A paragraph should normally create the reason for the next paragraph instead of ending as an isolated information block.

### 6. Title/opening payoff
The opening must promptly pay off the strongest title promise. A curiosity gap is legal only when the article can close it with evidence. Misleading omission, unsupported superlatives and bait-and-switch are failures.

## Headline candidate generation
For each applicable formal mode, generate 8–12 genuinely different candidates before selecting. Candidate diversity should come from different supported stakes/angles, not synonym swaps.

A selected title must have:
- one primary finance-wide click reason;
- an identifiable topic/entity;
- at least one current search-intent term considered, with evidence reference(s);
- an information gap the first three sentences can start paying off;
- factual strength no greater than the evidence.

## Completion design
Do not let the article “wind down” halfway through. The final substantive section must still add information, resolve an earlier question, establish a validation/falsification condition or close the causal chain. Avoid mechanical “总结” sections that merely repeat prior paragraphs.

Risk/disclaimer remains concise, topic-specific and materially complete so it does not become an unnecessarily long dead zone before the end of the page.

## Audit evidence
A PASS must cite the final selected title, the actual first three sentences, representative H1s and concrete transition evidence. “吸引人”“有钩子” without text-level evidence is not an audit.
