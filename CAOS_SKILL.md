# Financial Writing — CAOS child-native business entry

**Parent:** `D1traverser-debug/cognition-agent-os@main`, opt-in interactive cognition, goal recovery, competing causal diagnosis, methods and independent outcome review. **Child:** `D1traverser-debug/financial-writing-agent@main`, sole authority for article task identity, user-specific requirements, source/economic facts, finance writing, historical state and publication readiness.

The parent MAY observe the child; parent thought, a Manifest, a GitHub success check and same-chat PASS are never child business permissions or complete publishing outcomes. The current parent `src/cognition_agent_os/domain_contract.py` consumes the three historical `.uaos/` *filenames* as a read-only compatibility wire, which now declares CAOS. It does not execute an article. The child-native optional `financial_writing_agent/caos_bridge.py` provides a guarded interface that requires independent grant verification, actual provider CAS and a second state read. Never treat a callback stub as deployed native CAOS integration. No mutation or private owner transcript may be mirrored into CAOS without separate authorization.

## Real article creation and quality

Research from prior verified method knowledge → value-chain/economic company universe → chronological primary sources and scope/freshness checks → contradictory companies/data → two stable evidence sweeps when material → detailed PrivateResearchBrief 1.1 → evidence-backed DecisionEnvelope → independent Research Audit where available.

A RESEARCH transition requires DecisionEnvelope and mandatory digest-bound PrivateResearchBrief; an EDITORIAL transition requires distinct product-specific StoryBlueprints; EDITORIAL_AUDIT needs source-compared originality and all eight PublicationFitness gates: `FACT_INTEGRITY`, `RETENTION`, `COMPANY_GROUNDING`, `STRUCTURE_NUMBERING`, `ORIGINALITY`, `AUTHORIAL_STYLE`, `COMPLIANCE`, `PAYOFF`. A PASS string is not a verified underlying claim. If a source cannot support a material dated fact, do not invent it.

Publication: Deep selects a financially decisive thesis and must comply with any additional admitted task-specific universe requirement; Broad is a separately designed reader-acquisition story, never just a truncated Deep; platform interaction also has its own content job. Event-led causal exposition uses `event → business mechanism → company exposure → operating/profit effect → reversal test`. Full company coverage in research is not proof of meaningful company comparison in prose. User-approved voice and real article evidence outrank generic AI-detector optimization.
