# Reader Value — Current Publishing Semantics

Status: `CURRENT_CANDIDATE_1.0`
Owner: `DOMAIN:financial_writing`

## Governing outcome

A Financial publication should give a real target reader a strong reason to enter, a clear path through the material, repeated information gain, evidence-bounded company distinctions and a payoff worth finishing for.

The product is not optimized for visible checklist compliance. Research/governance complexity should disappear behind the article while continuing to protect factual and financial boundaries.

## Promoted semantics

### Research breadth vs publication selectivity

Research must cover the full material value chain before thesis selection. Publication may select only after that cognition exists. `FINANCE_DEEP` normally carries all material value-chain branches; `FINANCE_BROAD` may select a smaller audited subset for acquisition.

### Deep completeness

For thematic/industry Deep work:
- material value-chain classes must be represented;
- material companies must be economically differentiated;
- mechanisms should be grounded in named companies/cases;
- H1 uses explicit Chinese numbering;
- no default word-count ceiling applies.

Long is acceptable when each section adds material information. Repetition is not completeness.

### Story architecture

Long-form writing requires a StoryBlueprint before prose. Choose structure based on material rather than a universal template. Open with a concrete fact/company conflict/rule/case/anomaly when appropriate, establish a meaningful information gap, give an early payoff and preserve a later payoff.

Each section must add a distinct reader job, evidence and company/case grounding where useful, then bridge into the next section. The ending closes the promised question or states the unresolved boundary.

### Reader retention

Retention is a full-article property, not a title or first-three-sentence score. A reader should encounter recurring reasons to continue: new facts, company divergence, counterexamples, consequences and partial payoffs.

Curiosity gaps are allowed only when concrete and honestly paid off. Clicks alone do not prove satisfaction or completion.

### Company decision utility

When two or more companies are material, the article should name meaningful relative distinctions — for example more direct exposure, faster realization, harder evidence, higher but less certain elasticity, heavier inventory/FX risk — when supported by evidence and change conditions.

This utility is analytical, not actionable. Buy/sell, timing, target, position sizing and covert equivalents remain prohibited.

### Natural authorial prose

Draft from audited material + author stance + StoryBlueprint + strongest user-owned voice evidence. Do not draft from a giant list of anti-AI rules.

Generic stage language (`值得关注`, `这意味着`, `问题在于`, repeated `不是X而是Y`, etc.) is inspected when clustered or unearned. It is not a phrase blacklist. The real defect is when scaffolding replaces facts, company evidence or reasoning.

### Originality

Research sources supply evidence, not reusable prose. Normalize source material into claims/notes before drafting, write from understanding and compare suspicious/distinctive passages back against material sources. Patchwriting/close paraphrase requires revision. Same-chat inspection does not justify a zero-plagiarism claim.

### Publication source privacy

Do not expose `研报指出/据报道/公开资料显示` solely to demonstrate research effort. Keep attribution when the actor/source identity is part of the fact or when quoting distinctive language. Audit retains full provenance.

### Evidence-backed review

Current publication acceptance requires text-level evidence for Fact Integrity, Retention, Company Grounding, Structure/Numbering, Originality, Authorial Style, Compliance and Payoff. A checklist of PASS words without excerpts/findings is invalid evidence.

### Assurance

Same-session critics remain `CHAT_SELF_REVIEW`. Distinct roles or prompts are not independent unless distinct execution evidence exists.

### Broad and interaction products

Broad is rebuilt for acquisition rather than shortened from Deep. Interaction posts are independently written from the audited evidence base and may not be long-form truncations or canned slang/question templates.

## User feedback and evolution

User rejection, edits and post-publication performance are high-value reader evidence. They trigger diagnosis and may create evolution candidates, but they are not automatically universal rules. Current authority changes only after classification, alternative comparison, regression/eval and owner-scoped promotion.

## Stop condition for publishing

The article is ready only when it has cleared evidence integrity and publication fitness at the assurance actually available. “I followed the prompt” or “the audit says PASS” is not a reader-value done condition by itself.
