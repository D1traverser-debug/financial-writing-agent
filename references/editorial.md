# Editorial module

## Ownership
Editorial owns **what the article must cover, what may be compressed, and the macro order of sections** after research is audited. It does not separately score sentence-level authorial flow (Style) or reader forward-pull (Reader Funnel).

`FINANCE_DEEP` and `FINANCE_BROAD` use the same audited evidence base but different expression contracts.

## Shared TopicDNA
Before mode-specific structuring, determine:
- what genuinely changed;
- the main economic transmission chain;
- the most consequential reader misjudgment;
- which company-comparison dimensions matter;
- the reader-value / finance-wide question that makes the topic worth attention.

Do not use shared `core_contradictions` or shared `compression_candidates` to govern FINANCE_DEEP.

## FINANCE_DEEP — MATERIAL_COMPLETE
FINANCE_DEEP must express every **material** branch in the bounded audited research universe that could change a financially literate reader's judgment.

It is **not** “pick two or three representative stocks and explain those deeply”. Depth means complete material coverage plus differentiated depth, not selective company omission.

The plan declares explicit `completeness_obligations`. Depending on the topic these may include:
- important event legs / chronology;
- economic/industrial transmission;
- material value-chain classes and business-stage differences;
- financial realization/sensitivity;
- counterevidence and `why_this / why_not`;
- validation/falsification conditions;
- material risk/boundaries.

### Company coverage obligations
For company/theme research, FINANCE_DEEP additionally declares `company_coverage_obligations` from the audited Universe Coverage Ledger.

- Every `MATERIAL` company must be named in the Deep article and its role/sensitivity/boundary must be intelligible.
- `SECONDARY` companies may be grouped by value-chain class when grouping does not erase a material distinction.
- `REFERENCE_ONLY` names do not create a mandatory article mention.
- No company/class may disappear merely because another name has easier disclosures, is more familiar, or fits the chosen narrative better.
- If the research universe is `BOUNDED_WITH_GAPS`, the article must preserve the boundary rather than imply exhaustive market coverage.

If `market_explanation_scope = REQUIRED`, completeness obligations additionally preserve material economic-benefit differences, competing market-selection explanations, confidence boundaries, continuation/reversal conditions and unresolved/falsification points.

If scope is `NOT_MATERIAL`, do not force market-selection language into the article merely because companies appear in the research universe.

FINANCE_DEEP may compress repetition, textbook background, synonym restatement and immaterial detail. It must not omit a material analytical branch or MATERIAL company merely for brevity or elegance.

## Company comparison decision map — comparison may be a primary analytical focus
A multi-company finance article is incomplete if it names every company but never resolves **how the companies differ economically**. When company choice or differentiation is a material reader question, build a comparison map before the conclusion. The comparison itself may occupy a major section or become one of the article's central analytical jobs when that is where the reader value lies.

Use evidence-bounded dimensions such as:
- **order visibility** — how much current demand is already visible in orders/contracts;
- **realization speed** — how quickly orders can plausibly move into delivery, revenue or cash flow;
- **earnings sensitivity** — how strongly project acceleration/slowdown changes utilization, margin or profit;
- **overseas exposure** — whether export/customer/project routes are already validated rather than merely possible;
- **technology optionality** — whether a newer technical route is already commercial, still demonstration-stage, or only a future option.

The map may state a **research order, evidence hierarchy, current-vs-later distinction, visibility-vs-sensitivity distinction, stronger/weaker evidence, or what would change the ranking**. It may explicitly emphasize the comparison when the evidence supports doing so. It must not translate those dimensions into executable trading instructions such as buy/sell, entry timing, target price, position sizing or equivalent euphemisms.

The intended reader takeaway is: *which operating thesis is more or less evidenced right now, why the companies differ, and what evidence would move a company between buckets*. Evidence-bounded comparison is useful financial analysis; the prohibited boundary is actionable trading instruction, not comparison itself.

## Stock-grounded validation / monitoring sections
When a Deep section tells the reader **what to watch, validate, falsify, track, or wait for next**, do not detach the checklist from the stock universe.

Map the material signal back to named stocks or a clearly identified value-chain class. Examples of legitimate mapping include:
- order/tender signal → which cable/turbine/foundation/installation companies have economic exposure;
- delivery/production signal → which names have capacity or order conversion sensitivity;
- price/margin signal → which companies/classes have the relevant earnings elasticity;
- overseas project progress → which companies have validated export routes or customer exposure.

A generic final checklist such as “看招标、看交付、看收入” after earlier stock discussion is incomplete if it does not tell the reader **which stocks/classes each signal changes**.

## FINANCE_BROAD — SELECTIVE_TRAFFIC
FINANCE_BROAD may select one to three `core_contradictions` / information gaps and declare compression candidates to reduce first-read cost.

Selective expression does not relax evidence standards. Every retained conclusion needs the facts, causal chain, important counterevidence and uncertainty boundary required to support it.

For multi-company topics, Broad may compress the detailed ledger, but it should still leave a simple, truthful differentiation such as “current order visibility / operating elasticity / overseas evidence / longer-term option” so the reader does not leave with an undifferentiated stock basket.

If it retains a market-selection explanation, preserve the applicable evidence grade and key counterevidence.

## Structure generation
Create at least two **topic-specific** candidate structures from the current TopicDNA before selecting one. Do not select from a canned outline catalog or rotate templates mechanically.

A good structure is the shortest section sequence that:
- discharges the current mode's coverage obligations;
- makes causal/comparative order intelligible;
- places necessary premises before dependent conclusions;
- avoids duplicate chapters that answer the same question;
- avoids adding a section only because similar past articles had one.

Recent accepted outlines may be used only as collision evidence to avoid repetitive structure, never as a template to copy.

## Macro structural coherence
Editorial checks section-level architecture:
- prerequisites appear before conclusions that depend on them;
- comparison dimensions are introduced before ranking/selection claims;
- important counterevidence is placed where it can genuinely affect interpretation;
- scope changes are explicit rather than abrupt;
- each major section has a distinct analytical job;
- **each major section leaves an earned reason for the next one**—cause, consequence, comparison, chronology, narrowing, correction or inference rather than an abrupt topic reset;
- Deep company coverage can be traced from the research ledger into the section plan.

Detailed paragraph-to-paragraph **argument progression** belongs to `style.md`. Whether H1s/transitions provide enough motivation to continue reading belongs to `hooks.md`. Do not triple-count the same defect.

## Numbering and no tables
Use continuous report-style prose with meaningful hierarchy when reasoning has **real** layers, categories, steps or parallel branches:
`一、二、三 → （1）（2）（3） → ①②③`.

For FINANCE_DEEP, lower-level numbering is still semantic rather than a fixed quota, but the default should be **to expose real branches rather than hide them in dense continuous prose**. Use `（1）（2）（3）` and `①②③` generously whenever they reduce cognitive load or make comparisons, stages, mechanisms, company classes, evidence grades or validation paths easier to scan.

In particular, a Deep section with several company classes, multiple realization stages, three or more meaningful comparison dimensions, or multiple causal branches should normally expose those branches with lower-level numbering. Do not wait for the prose to become unreadable before using hierarchy.

A section may remain continuous prose when its reasoning is genuinely sequential and gains nothing from subdivision. Do not force every H1 to have the same number of children or manufacture empty subpoints for visual symmetry. The number and depth of subpoints follow the actual logic.

Navigation quality is not monotonic in raw heading count: excessive symmetric numbering can still become a defect when it creates fake structure. The governing preference is **more visible hierarchy for real analytical structure, less decorative hierarchy for nonexistent structure**. Publication articles do not use tables; convert necessary comparison material into prose whose hierarchy follows the argument.

## Information density
There is no default word-count target. Length follows material information density.

FINANCE_DEEP compression cannot remove a branch/company that could change judgment. FINANCE_BROAD can compress additional non-essential branches when retained claims stay fully supported.

## Reader Funnel / Hook handoff
Load `hooks.md` for title/opening/H1/continuation/completion design. Editorial supplies the material and macro architecture; Reader Funnel judges qualified reader value and forward pull.

Formal modes generate 8–12 genuinely different title candidates from supported reader-value propositions. A valid pool must represent multiple strategy families, not one rhetorical mold with synonyms. For `FINANCE_BROAD`, the article should normally expose a non-niche financial stake so a cold finance reader does not need prior sector interest. Broader unrelated macro relevance remains optional.

## Natural Authorial Prose handoff
Load `style.md` for fidelity, semantic contribution, argument progression, reader calibration, authorial selectivity, evidence specificity, natural structural rhythm, template control and calibrated rhetoric.

Do not use “去AI味” as a separate editorial objective. Repeated staging phrases or symmetrical section molds are a real structural defect when they cluster, even if every individual sentence is grammatical.

## Bold skim-path content selection
All A-share company names supplied to the publication document are bolded deterministically by Delivery. That fixed entity emphasis **does not replace semantic emphasis**.

Editorial must still choose a useful set of key facts, numbers, mechanisms and judgment sentences for explicit `**...**` emphasis. The final **title + H1s + all bold spans** should recover the main line: what changed, the core transmission chain, the important company comparison and the conclusion boundary. Because A-share names are always bold, the explicit semantic anchors are especially important to prevent the skim path from degrading into a mere list of companies.

## Risk section
The writer returns `risk_disclaimer`; deterministic delivery appends it as the final inline italic `风险提示：...` paragraph. Do not place a duplicate risk/disclaimer H1 inside article blocks.

The risk paragraph should be **concise but information-complete** for the current topic. Do not pad it to satisfy a character-count quota; do not shorten it by deleting a material risk/boundary merely to look concise.

## General editorial judge
General editorial audit is a distinct judgment responsibility from Reader Funnel and Natural Authorial Prose. This separation is conceptual and rubric-level; it does not imply a physically independent executor unless separate provenance is actually present. It checks:
- evidence/mode contract;
- FINANCE_DEEP material and company-universe completeness or FINANCE_BROAD legal compression;
- stock-grounded validation/monitoring sections;
- company-comparison decision usefulness, including whether comparison deserves to be a major analytical focus, while preserving the non-actionable boundary;
- Market Explanation discipline when scope is REQUIRED;
- macro structural coherence, earned major-section bridges and semantically justified navigation when branching;
- information density;
- skim-path substance beyond automatic A-share-name emphasis;
- compliance and concise-but-complete risk boundaries.

General editorial PASS is illegal until the dedicated Reader Funnel and Authorial Prose gates PASS.
