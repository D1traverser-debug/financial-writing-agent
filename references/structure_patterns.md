# Structure pattern library

These are **design patterns, not article templates**. Select or combine only after TopicDNA is formed. Do not rotate them mechanically.

## 1. Contradiction-first
Use when one factual contradiction explains why the market's first interpretation is incomplete.

Typical motion: apparent fact → conflicting fact → resolve contradiction → company implications → verification.

## 2. Causal-chain
Use when the investment conclusion depends on several transmission steps.

Typical motion: catalyst → industry mechanism → price/order/profit/cash-flow transmission → company differences → market selection.

## 3. Competition / elimination
Use when many companies map to the same event and the key question is why only a few become market leaders.

Typical motion: full universe → comparison dimensions → eliminate weak mappings → why_this / why_not → market role → falsification.

## 4. Policy-to-cash-flow
Use when a policy headline is broad but the economic value depends on financing, pricing, payment, project conversion or company cash flow.

Typical motion: policy wording → implementation mechanism → who pays / who earns → order and profit timing → listed-company mapping.

## 5. Expectation-vs-reality
Use when price action, valuation, narrative or consensus has moved ahead of business data.

Typical motion: market expectation → operating reality → gap → what must happen to close the gap → company ranking / risks.

## 6. Phase-transition / timeline
Use when the core insight is that a sector has moved from one development stage to another.

Typical motion: old stage → new evidence → transition threshold → changed KPI → changed company hierarchy → next validation point.

## Selection discipline

- Produce at least two candidate structures from the current TopicDNA.
- Pattern names are internal aids and need not appear in the article.
- Hybrid structures are allowed when they improve causal clarity.
- After candidates exist, compare against recent outline fingerprints only for collision detection.
- Reject any structure that adds a chapter merely because a pattern contains it.
