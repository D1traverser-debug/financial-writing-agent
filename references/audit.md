# Audit module

## Independence
The final auditor re-evaluates the frozen final artifact/state. Writer self-assessment is not evidence of passing.

## Mechanical gates
Use deterministic code where possible for required artifacts, delivery channel, file identity, Word styles/numbering, stock-name bolding, unambiguous actionable red-line phrases, risk/disclaimer presence/minimum substance, expected embedded image count and successful render output.

## Semantic gates
Use an independent judge for evidence support/source boundary; reasoning continuity/transitions; negative evidence and why-this/why-not; title truthfulness/opening payoff; information density/structure-topic fit; platform fit; anti-AI style; unsupported extreme language; context-sensitive compliance; risk-warning information quality; visual/article semantic alignment and avatar treatment when visuals are enabled; and remaining uncertainty.

A successful render process is not itself visual proof of no clipping or bad pagination. Claim visual page inspection only when the rendered pages were actually made available to and viewed by the auditor.

## Audit report
Generate `WRITING_AUDIT_REPORT.docx` from final state. Include task contract/distribution, source/evidence boundary, research coverage, stage-by-stage PASS/REVISE/FAIL/N/A, mechanical/semantic results, defects/fixes, remaining boundaries, knowledge reuse/writeback references when available, visual manifest identity when applicable and final artifact identities.

A missing audit report is a completion failure under `WORD_FILE_ONLY`.

## Visible checklist
The final chat response must include a concise checklist status covering runtime/gates, research/evidence, structure/style/continuity, Word/render, compliance/risk disclaimer, visuals if applicable, and knowledge reuse/writeback if applicable. Under `WORD_FILE_ONLY` this checklist must not paste the article body.

## Fail closed
No artifact may be COMPLETE while an applicable Must-Pass item lacks evidence. Changes to facts, title, opening, H1 structure, body logic, visual placement/assets or formatting invalidate affected checks and require re-audit.
