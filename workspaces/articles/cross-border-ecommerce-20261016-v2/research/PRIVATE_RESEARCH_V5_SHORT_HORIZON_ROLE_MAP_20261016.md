## Short-horizon role ontology used in V5

- Emotional high mark / space board: opens market or theme height; not necessarily the purest industrial beneficiary.
- Theme overall leader: combines recognition, persistence, leadership and peer spillover; need not equal the market's highest board.
- Pioneer: earliest instrument to price a new catalyst; may later lose leadership.
- Capacity middle army / trend core: larger-cap, high-turnover instrument that can absorb institutional/large capital and confirm that the theme is broader than small-cap speculation.
- Branch leader: core of a specific benefit chain after a large catalyst expands into B2B, overseas warehouse/logistics, payment, marketing, sellers/manufacturing, etc.
- Carding / positional replacement: a relative competition concept. It requires a concrete peer/opponent and a role vacancy; faster sealing, stronger resealing, better carry or harder logic can replace the prior core. Do not label every strong stock as 'carding'.
- Support: same-day or adjacent move that confirms theme breadth and supports the core.
- Catch-up: lower-position instrument discovered after the leader has opened height and created a profit effect; it normally does not perform first-round price discovery.
- Height remapping: an existing high-position stock from another narrative may be reinterpreted under a new catalyst, but this is admitted only if the new theme's peer branches co-move.

## Catalyst severity

- S: sudden + official/explicit + changes profitability + covers multiple links. Can naturally create pioneer -> leader/high mark -> capacity core -> branch leaders -> support -> catch-up ladder.
- A: explicit industrial catalyst with narrower coverage. Usually one main branch plus capacity/limited catch-up.
- B: expected event, operational data or known validation window. More selective; true orders/revenue matter more than labels.
- C: old news, interaction-board material, weak concept tag. Usually isolated board/one-day trade unless the sentiment cycle itself amplifies it.

Catalyst severity must be multiplied by the market sentiment cycle. A strong policy in a retreat cycle can still produce only one pioneer plus one capacity core; an old theme in a climax can temporarily broaden, but weak tags are usually first to fail in divergence.
