## Why V3 was insufficient

A broad company universe and static business classification were necessary but not sufficient for recommendation-grade research. The missing layer was pre-event market selection logic: why capital would choose company A rather than company B under the same theme, how current market regime affects that choice, and what next-session evidence would validate or falsify the thesis.

## Reference patterns absorbed

From the reviewed Jiuyangongshe consecutive-board recap and the user's prior 260924 recap, the useful grain was abstracted into the following fields rather than copied as article structure:

1. market regime and sentiment structure;
2. catalyst timing and catalyst type: new information / old information re-trade / expectation / realization;
3. business-realization stage;
4. why this company rather than peers;
5. earnings elasticity source;
6. current board-position / recent limit-up memory / float / turnover / board quality;
7. sector linkage and leader-middle-follow structure;
8. negative pool and likely falsification cases;
9. cross-sectional counterfactual comparison;
10. next-session validation conditions and scenario tree.

## Business-realization stage used in V4

- S5 SCALE_CORE: cross-border business is a major revenue/profit engine with quantifiable data.
- S4 OPERATING_EXPANSION: real and growing cross-border business, not yet dominant.
- S3 SMALL_SCALE_LIVE: customers/channels/orders/flows exist but remain small.
- S2 PILOT_EXPANSION: product/channel/overseas node exists with limited financial contribution.
- S1 PLAN_AGREEMENT_EQUITY: narrative exists but no verified revenue contribution.
- S0 LABEL_ONLY: market-data concept tag without a verified profit-transmission chain.

-> VALIDATION / FALSIFICATION

## Current market-regime correction

As of 2026-10-16 close, public limit-up recap data show an 11-board market height and a roughly 13.3% 1-to-2-board promotion rate. This changes the pre-trade interpretation from a generic low-height mixed regime to one where very high leaders can hold together while low-position follow-through is selective. The cross-border theme should therefore be judged by cross-branch confirmation rather than by one isolated limit-up.
