## Selected title
**AI越烧钱，防火墙反而越值钱：网安正在变成AI基础设施的“安全税”**

## Selection rationale
- Contains a concrete contradiction (AI capex vs firewall), a financial stake (security spend), and a real analytical distinction (infrastructure tax rather than broad cybersecurity recovery).
- Passes cold-reader test: a general finance reader can infer that AI capex is redirecting enterprise spending even without prior cybersecurity knowledge.
- Avoids claiming that all domestic AI-security orders are already recognized revenue.
- Compatible with contradiction opening and the selected editorial structure.
- Search terms remain natural in title/body rather than stuffed.
